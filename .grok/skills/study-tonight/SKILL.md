# study-tonight

Termwise skill. Use when the student asks what to work on tonight.

## Steps

1. Look at confirmed calendar items due today through the next 4 days.
2. Include suggested study blocks only if the student kept them.
3. List course, item, hours if known, and why it is tonight.
4. Do not invent due dates or grades.

## Safety

Show the checklist first. Do not write calendar events unless the student confirms.
